4. Specific Trading Risks

4.1 Leverage Risk

Trading on margin (using leverage) increases both potential profits and potential losses. A relatively small market movement can have a proportionately larger impact on your account. You may lose more than your initial investment.

4.2 Market Risk

Market prices can be volatile and unpredictable. News events, economic data, political developments, and other factors can cause rapid price movements.

4.3 Liquidity Risk

In volatile or illiquid markets, you may be unable to close positions at desired prices, potentially resulting in significant losses.

4.4 Gap Risk

Markets can gap significantly, especially over weekends or during major news events, causing positions to be filled at prices far from your stop-loss orders.

4.5 Counterparty Risk

Your broker or trading platform may become insolvent or fail to honor obligations, potentially resulting in loss of funds.

4.6 Technology Risk

Technical failures, internet outages, or platform issues may prevent you from managing positions or executing trades.

4.7 Cryptocurrency Specific Risks

Cryptocurrency markets involve additional risks:

  • Extreme volatility (price swings of 10%+ in a day are common)
  • Limited regulatory oversight
  • Exchange hacks and security breaches
  • Regulatory uncertainty and potential bans
  • Irreversible transactions
  • Market manipulation
