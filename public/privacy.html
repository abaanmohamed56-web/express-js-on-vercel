Privacy Policy

Last Updated: February 15, 2025

1. Introduction

Skill Pips ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our website, services, and educational content.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

We collect information that you voluntarily provide to us, including:

  • Account Information: Name, email address, password
  • Payment Information: Billing details (processed securely through Stripe or NOWPayments)
  • Telegram Information: Telegram username and user ID (for VIP group access)
  • Communication Data: Information from your interactions with support

2.2 Automatically Collected Information

When you access our Services, we may automatically collect:

  • IP address and location data
  • Browser type and version
  • Device information
  • Pages visited and time spent on pages
  • Referring website
  • Cookies and similar tracking technologies

2.3 Third-Party Data

We may receive information from third-party services:

  • Stripe: Payment processing and subscription management data
  • NOWPayments: Cryptocurrency payment confirmation data
  • Vercel/DigitalOcean: Hosting and analytics data

3. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information for the following purposes:

  • Service Delivery: To provide access to courses and VIP community
  • Account Management: To create and maintain your account
  • Payment Processing: To process transactions and manage subscriptions
  • Communication: To send service updates, renewal reminders, and support responses
  • Improvement: To analyze usage and improve our Services
  • Security: To detect fraud and ensure platform security
  • Legal Compliance: To comply with legal obligations

4. Information Sharing and Disclosure

4.1 We Share Information With:

  • Payment Processors: Stripe and NOWPayments for payment processing
  • Hosting Providers: Vercel and DigitalOcean for service hosting
  • Email Service: SendGrid for transactional emails
  • Telegram: To provide VIP group access (username and ID only)

4.2 We Do Not:

  • Sell your personal information to third parties
  • Share your information for marketing purposes
  • Disclose your trading activity or personal data publicly

4.3 Legal Requirements

We may disclose your information if required by law, court order, or governmental regulation.

5. Data Security

We implement security measures to protect your information:

  • SSL/TLS encryption for all data transmission
  • Encrypted password storage
  • Secure payment processing through PCI-compliant providers
  • Regular security audits and updates
  • Access controls and authentication

However, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as:

  • Your account is active
  • Needed to provide Services
  • Required by law (typically 7 years for financial records)

After account deletion, we may retain anonymized data for analytics.

7. Your Privacy Rights

7.1 General Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal data
  • Correction: Update inaccurate information
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your data (subject to legal requirements)
  • Portability: Receive your data in a structured format
  • Object: Object to certain data processing activities
  • Withdraw Consent: Withdraw consent for data processing

7.2 GDPR Rights (EU Residents)

If you are in the European Union, you have additional rights under GDPR including the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

7.3 CCPA Rights (California Residents)

California residents have the right to request disclosure of personal data collection and sales (we do not sell personal data).

7.4 How to Exercise Your Rights

Contact us at privacy@skillpips.com to exercise any of these rights. We will respond within 30 days.

8. Cookies and Tracking

8.1 Types of Cookies We Use

  • Essential Cookies: Required for site functionality (login, checkout)
  • Analytics Cookies: To understand how visitors use our site
  • Preference Cookies: To remember your settings and preferences

8.2 Cookie Control

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Note that disabling certain cookies may limit functionality.

9. Third-Party Links

Our Services may contain links to external websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these third-party sites. We encourage you to read their privacy policies.

10. Children's Privacy

Our Services are not intended for individuals under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If we discover we have collected data from a child, we will delete it promptly.

11. International Data Transfers

Your information may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your country of residence. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place for such transfers in compliance with applicable laws.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes via email or through our Services. The "Last Updated" date will be revised accordingly.

13. Do Not Track Signals

Some browsers have a "Do Not Track" feature. We currently do not respond to Do Not Track signals.

14. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, contact us at:

Email: privacy@skillpips.com
Support: support@skillpips.com
Data Protection Officer: dpo@skillpips.com

🔒 Your Privacy Matters

We are committed to protecting your privacy and handling your data responsibly. If you have any concerns about how we handle your information, please don't hesitate to contact us.
